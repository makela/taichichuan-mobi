## Account

- You register with your email address and log in with links sent to it.
- An account is for one person. Don't let others use your account.
- The free lessons and practices are open to everyone registered.

## Membership

- A membership opens the members' lessons in order, the practices, and the
  following sets as you finish the earlier ones.
- It is paid in advance, monthly or yearly, through Stripe, and renews
  automatically until you cancel it. The price is shown before you pay.
- You can cancel at any time on the account page, under "Manage membership
  and payments". The membership then lasts until the end of the period you
  have paid for. Periods already begun are not refunded, unless the law
  requires it.
- **Right of withdrawal:** as a consumer you would usually have 14 days to
  withdraw from a purchase. The membership opens the lessons at once, at your
  request: when joining, you agree to this and acknowledge that you then lose
  the right of withdrawal.
- Price changes are told by email at least 30 days before they apply, and
  they apply from your next period. You can cancel before that.

- **Downloading, recording, copying or sharing the videos or texts, or sharing

## Practise safely

Tai Chi is physical exercise. Practise within your own limits, and ask a
doctor first if you have health problems or are unsure. The lessons do not
replace medical advice. You practise at your own risk, as far as the law
allows.

## Changes and breaks

The lessons are developed and can change. The service aims to be available at
all times, but there may be breaks. Changes to these terms are told by email
before they apply; if you don't accept them, you can cancel your membership and
delete your account.

## Ending

You can delete your account at any time on the account page, which also ends
your membership. Taiji-Timo may close an account that breaks these terms.

## Law and disputes

These terms follow Finnish law. If something goes wrong, please contact
Taiji-Timo first. As a consumer you can also take a dispute to the Consumer
Disputes Board, [kuluttajariita.fi](https://www.kuluttajariita.fi/en), after
contacting the consumer advisory services. These terms don't limit your rights
as a consumer.
