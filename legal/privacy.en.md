This notice tells what personal data the Taiji-Timo lessons at
taichichuan.mobi handle, why, and what your rights are. The front page alone
collects no personal data.

## Controller

Taiji-Timo, business ID 2212553-3, Finland\
Email: taichiwayfarer@gmail.com

## What data, and why

- **Account:** your email address, the language you chose, and when you
  registered. They are used to log you in by emailed links and to give you the
  lessons. Basis: the contract between us (your account and these terms).
- **Progress:** which lessons you have passed, and when. It is used to open
  the lessons in order. Basis: contract.
- **Membership:** until when your membership is paid, and the identifiers of
  your customer and subscription at Stripe. Basis: contract.
- **Payments:** you pay on Stripe's own page. Card details go to Stripe only;
  Taiji-Timo never sees them. Stripe keeps payment records as the law
  requires. Basis: contract and the duty to keep accounts.
- **Logging in:** a single cookie keeps you logged in for 30 days or until you
  log out. It is needed for the service, so no consent is asked for it. Login
  links work once, within 30 minutes. Only scrambled forms of the secrets in
  cookies and links are stored.
- **Server logs:** the web server logs requests, including IP addresses, to
  keep the service secure and to find faults. They are kept for at most 30
  days. Basis: legitimate interest.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no data is sold or
used for marketing.

## Who else handles the data

- **Stripe** (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) handles the payments, see
  [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **Google** (Gmail) sends the login emails. Google may handle data outside
  the EU and EEA, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European
  Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long the data is kept

- Your account, progress and membership data are kept until you delete your
  account, which you can do at any time on the account page. Deleting removes
  them at once and deletes your customer data at Stripe; only the payment
  records that bookkeeping law requires are kept, for the time it requires.
- Login links are removed once used, or after 30 minutes. A login ends after
  30 days, or when you log out.

## Your rights

You have the right to see the data about you, to have it corrected or
deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to get it in a machine-readable
form. You can delete your account yourself; for anything else, send an email.

If you think your data is handled against the law, you can complain to the
Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland,
[tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en).
